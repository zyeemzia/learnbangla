# Learn Bangla · সহজে বাংলা শিখি

Easy Bangla for English speakers: read the script, speak with people, enjoy the culture.
One set of content powers two outputs: an interactive web app and a printable book.

**Live site: https://learnbangla.zyeemzia.com/**

## Open it

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Web app | https://learnbangla.zyeemzia.com/ (installs an offline cache on first visit) |
| Printable book | Open `book.html` → Ctrl + P → Save as PDF / print (A4, background graphics on) |
| Ready PDF | `Learn-Bangla-Book.pdf` |

## What's inside

6 units · 59 lessons · 397 quiz questions · 280 vocabulary words · 92 word-by-word sentences · 24 conversations

1. **Bangla Script**: Vowels, consonants, vowel signs, conjuncts and numerals
2. **Writing Bangla**: Pen grip, letters, vowel signs, conjuncts and numerals by hand
3. **First Words & Phrases**: Greetings, numbers, money, family, food and getting around
4. **Grammar Made Easy**: Word order, respect, tenses, questions and the little endings that hold Bangla together
5. **Everyday Conversations**: Real-life Bangla for the bazaar, the road, the table, the phone and festivals
6. **Reading & Culture**: Graded stories, the national anthem, proverbs and the culture behind the language

**Web app extras:** built-in natural Bangla voices (female and male) for every word and sentence, so pronunciation works on every device with nothing to install,
a writing studio (watch-the-pen demos and tracing pads with star scores, dictation),
daily missions with XP and streaks, word-by-word sentences with a memorise mode, conversations with play-all and role-play,
Letter Hunt, Listen & Pick, Word Match, Sentence Builder and Speed Quiz games, word families,
spaced-repetition flashcards, a downloadable certificate, a sound setup guide for every device,
light and dark theme and adjustable Bangla text size.

**Book extras:** every lesson with copybook writing pages,
word-search and sentence puzzles, word families, a journal, answer keys, a progress tracker and a certificate.

Fonts are bundled in `fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License.
